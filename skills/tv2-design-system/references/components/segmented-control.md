## Appearance

Preview shows two text options inside a single muted dark pill-shaped container. The left option sits on a white inner pill, inset slightly from the container edge, with dark centered text. The right option has white centered text directly on the shared dark surface. Both options occupy the same horizontal row and have aligned label centers. The enclosing track and inner selected pill distinguish this composition from Button Group's separate pills.

## Recorded rules

The On Image variant uses primitive white for text and icons in both light and dark themes, corrected in version 1.1.0. The visible example contains text only. Its white selected fill does not establish the On Image variant's full appearance.
