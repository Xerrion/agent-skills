## Appearance

Preview shows three filled circles in a horizontal row, aligned through their centers. The first circle is white and larger than the two muted gray circles that follow it. The row has no enclosing visible surface or text. The difference in size and brightness distinguishes the emphasized position from its neighbors.

## Recorded rules

The component height is 12 px, changed from 10 px to follow the four-point system. Each dot occupies a square area, instead of using gaps to position the dots, so state changes keep the layout stable. The record specifies semantic tokens without naming an exact dot diameter in the log.
