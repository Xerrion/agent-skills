## Appearance

Preview shows three evenly arranged value-and-unit columns in one horizontal group. Large light-weight numbers form the top row; smaller light-weight unit labels sit directly below each value. Each unit is centered under its number: "timer", "min", and "sek". All text is white on the dark canvas. There are no visible separators, boxes, or surrounding pill.

## Recorded rules

Both numbers and text use the light font weight. Countdown has static Default and Small sizes plus a separate TV size. The source relates Default countdown to Default button; its Small line repeats "Small countdown". It gives no numeric countdown height or text size, and it predates the button's later size and naming changes. Keep this sizing relation separate from the current numeric button rules.
