## Appearance

Preview shows a wide rectangular header with square-looking corners and a muted dark surface against the darker canvas. The white text sits at the left; a white downward chevron sits near the right edge. Both align around the same horizontal centerline, with inset space at either end. The header carries a single line of text, and the preview shows no expanded content.

## Recorded rules

Version 1.1.0 specifies a 12 px gap between text and icon and supports two text lines. Version 2.0.0 removes the transparent variant. The Figma Slot property replaces the earlier slot workaround; it does not specify the appearance of expanded content.
