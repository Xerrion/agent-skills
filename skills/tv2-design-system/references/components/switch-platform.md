## Appearance

Preview shows a compact horizontal capsule with a bright green filled track. A white circular thumb sits toward the right, inset from the end, leaving more exposed track on the left. The thumb is smaller than the track's height, with a visible green margin above and below it. There is no visible label or icon. This is the depicted right-thumb state; the preview does not show its opposite state.

## Recorded rules

The source describes minimal styling of Material 3 and Liquid Glass switches. Version 2.0.0 rebuilds the Figma switch with components instead of modes. The earlier record says the On color can be changed for Apple, while its Off color was unresolved. The preview's green is not a universal track-color token for every platform, theme, or state.
