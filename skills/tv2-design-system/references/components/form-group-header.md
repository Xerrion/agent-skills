## Appearance

Preview shows three stacked text levels. A large bold heading sits first, followed by a large regular-weight description and a smaller muted required marker in parentheses. The three lines share a left edge. A filled light circle with a dark information glyph sits directly after the heading on the same row. It belongs to the heading row rather than the description or required marker.

The example has no enclosing panel or divider. The required marker is visually secondary through both size and color. It sits on a separate line, so it remains distinct from the group description.
