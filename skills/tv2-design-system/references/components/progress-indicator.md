## Appearance

Preview shows a linear indicator and a circular indicator as two separate examples. The linear form is a thin horizontal track with rounded outer ends: its leading portion is white and its remaining portion is muted gray. It has no thumb or text. The circular form is a ring with a dark open center, a white arc, and a muted gray arc. The ring sits below the line in the preview; this arrangement is not a required combined component.
