## Appearance

Preview shows a pale orange rectangular notice with square corners and dark content. A warning triangle sits at the upper left. To its right, a heavier two line title sits above a smaller explanatory paragraph. The paragraph wraps within the text column, and both text blocks share a left edge. A circular close control occupies a separate area at the upper right, aligned with the title region. Its fill is slightly darker than the banner surface, with a dark cross at its center. The panel has inset space around the content and enough room beside the text for both controls.
