# Epic

An Epic describes a larger outcome, significant deliverable, or capability with multiple meaningful child outcomes.
Describe the result and scope, not a list of implementation steps. Make success measurable without inventing targets.
Stories, Tasks, and Bugs can contribute to the Epic. Propose children only when they form useful planning or delivery units.
Do not require the Epic itself to fit within one sprint.

## Template

```markdown
## Objective

<High-level capability or outcome>

## Problem

<What larger problem needs a solution?>

## Desired Outcome

<What should be possible when this Epic is complete?>

## Scope

### In Scope

- <Capability>

### Out of Scope

- <Supported exclusion>

## Acceptance Criteria

- <Measurable outcome>

## Dependencies

- <Known material dependency>

## References

<Designs, decision records, documentation, or related initiatives>
```
